# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback,
and do not harass, insult or attack anyone, publicly or privately.

It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior is unacceptable, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@Piekario](https://github.com/Piekario), by opening a
[private report](https://github.com/Piekario/wow-mcp/security/advisories/new) titled "Code of conduct", or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions and may
block people who break this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
